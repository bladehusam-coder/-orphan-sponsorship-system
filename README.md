# Orphan Sponsorship & Charity Accounting Ecosystem 🌍❤️

An integrated, open-source platform designed to empower non-profit organizations and charities with complete financial transparency, structured case management, and seamless donor engagement.

## 🚀 Core Modules

1. **Orphan & Critical Cases Database:**
   - Secure, structured profiles for orphans and vulnerable families.
   - Tracking educational, medical, and financial needs over time.
   - Privacy-first architecture with role-based access control (RBAC).

2. **Comprehensive Charity Fund Accounting:**
   - Double-entry bookkeeping system tailored specifically for non-profits.
   - Restricted vs. unrestricted fund tracking to guarantee donor money reaches its exact intended purpose.
   - Automated financial reporting and audit trails.

3. **Sponsor Web & Mobile Portal:**
   - User-friendly dashboard for sponsors to browse cases and track updates.
   - Secure recurring payment gateway integration for monthly sponsorships.
   - Automated receipts and impact reports.

## 🌐 Localization & Accessibility
- Built from the ground up with full **Bilingual Support (Arabic RTL & English LTR)** to serve humanitarian organizations in the Middle East and globally.
